# Codex handoff: player weapon images

Four images. Style reference is art/ace.png. Save each as 1024x1024 PNG at the path given.

## Option A: one command (non-interactive)

From the creative/ folder, after codex login:

    node art/codex-weapon-image.mjs --all

Add --assembled for the turret check shot, --force to overwrite.

## Option B: paste into an interactive Codex session

Start codex in creative/ and paste everything below the line.

---

Use your image generation tool. Attach and study art/ace.png first: every image must match its art style, materials, camera, and lighting exactly. Generate FOUR separate images and save each one as a 1024x1024 PNG at the exact path in its heading. Do not write or edit any other files. After each save, continue to the next. When all four exist, reply with the four paths.

Hard rules across all four:
- The Gimbal Base has exactly ONE gold part, the round collar at the bottom.
- The three gun cartridges have ZERO gold and NO collar, ring, or pedestal under them. Each has a charcoal trunnion block at the rear with a cream hexagonal axle stub sticking out of each side.
- Each gun has exactly FOUR empty hexagonal crystal wells. The base has none.
- Clean light-gray studio background, nothing else in frame.

### 1. art/weapons/gimbal-base.png

3D game-ready concept of a SINGLE standalone spaceship weapon TURRET BASE with NO gun installed, isolated on a clean light-gray studio background. No ship, no hangar, no scenery.

CAMERA: high 3/4 isometric from above-front-right, same product-shot camera as the crimson Ace interceptor catalog image. Entire part fully in frame, centered, no cropping. Slight downward angle like a top-down arcade game. Orthographic isometric product shot.

STYLE: Same locked ART FAMILY as Ace — StarCraft Terran + Ratchet & Clank / Fortnite / Astroneer toyetic. Bright colors, slightly low-poly, thick panel lines, chunky readable shapes, faint edge wear. SAME materials language as Ace: crimson-red plates, cream/white plates, charcoal mechanical recesses, stylized toy look. NOT photoreal, NOT grimdark.

PART IDENTITY: Gimbal Base. ONE-LINE SILHOUETTE: a short gold collar, a charcoal rotating ring, and an empty crimson U-shaped yoke on top with two cream pivot caps and nothing between them. It is a mount waiting for a gun. If it has a barrel, coils, missiles, or anything pointing forward, it is wrong.

SHAPE, from the bottom up: (1) Circular GOLD mounting collar at the very bottom, the only gold on the whole object. Underside has a dark hexagonal plug that seats into a ship's gold hardpoint pad. (2) On top of the collar: a slightly wider CHARCOAL yaw ring, a flat turntable with small cream index tick marks around its rim like a compass ring, and a thin visible seam so it reads as something that spins a full 360 degrees. (3) On the turntable: a squat crimson-red drum housing with a small charcoal vent. (4) Rising from the drum: a crimson-red U-shaped YOKE, two short thick vertical arms, cream cheek plates on the outside of each arm. (5) Near the top of each arm, on the inside face, a round CREAM trunnion cap with a hexagonal socket in its center. These two caps face each other and share one horizontal axis. (6) Between the arms: an EMPTY dark charcoal cradle, open at the front and top, with a small crimson locking clamp folded back. Nothing sits in the cradle. Overall about as tall as it is wide, compact enough for a fighter wing pad. Chunky toy proportions, slightly low-poly, thick panel lines.

GOLD RULE: gold exists ONLY as the single circular mounting collar at the very bottom. The yaw ring is charcoal, not gold. Trunnion caps are cream, not gold. No gold rings, no gold screws.

NO MOD WELLS: this base has zero hexagonal crystal wells. Wells live on the gun cartridges, not the base.

Lighting: bright studio key, soft fill, punchy rim light. Low-poly stylized 3D product render.

DO NOT INCLUDE: a gun, a barrel, coils, missiles, rockets, muzzle glow, a spaceship, a second part, hangar, stars, nebula, planet, text, logo, watermark, photorealism, grimdark, people, hands, extra gold circles, crystal wells, cropped part.

WEAPON IDENTITY: Laser Cannon. A long forward cream barrel with charcoal cooling fins along the sides, ending in a single circular muzzle. Inside the barrel and at the muzzle: hot orange energy glow (warm orange like Ace's engine bells, RGB-ish 255, 56, 4). Small charcoal heat vents. The rear of the receiver is short and rounded off. Thick panel lines. Chunky toy proportions, slightly low-poly.

MOD SLOTS: exactly 4 empty hexagonal crystal wells in a row on top of the crimson receiver. Cream bezels, dark empty glass, nothing inserted. These wells are NOT gold. Count them: 4 empty wells.

NO GOLD RULE: there is zero gold on this object. No gold collar, no gold ring, no gold barrel bands, no gold screws. The mount is cream axle stubs on a charcoal block.

Lighting: bright studio key, soft fill, punchy rim light. Low-poly stylized 3D product render.
