# The Equation — larger cold iron pass

Third concept pass: fifteen constructs one size class above the compact drones
in [`../cold-iron/`](../cold-iron/README.md). They retain the same unpainted
metal, exposed-power palette, deliberate asymmetry, and single shared weapon
mount.

These are compact fighter- or gunship-scale machines, not crewed ships. No form
has a cockpit, canopy, windows, pilot volume, face, or conventional aircraft
requirement.

## Composite subset

Three concepts test a larger unit assembled from smaller machines:

- **Needle / Constellation** — seven crescent drones interlock around one lance.
- **Bastion / Cohort** — four blunt unarmed orbs dock around one cannon core.
- **Relay / Chorus** — nine unarmed relay cells form one network coordinator.

The component drones remain recognizable and are joined with mechanical docking
collars plus energy bridges. Once assembled, they expose exactly one weapon for
the entire construct; component hardpoints are capped.

## Needle — larger saturation unit

- **Corkscrew** (`needle-corkscrew.png`): incomplete helical armor wraps a
  central shaft and cell bank; one forward lance.
- **Pike** (`needle-pike.png`): nested offset shells form a deep pike head around
  one energized tip.
- **Constellation** (`needle-constellation.png`) — *composite*: seven crescent
  drones dock into a spiral cluster around one central lance.

## Vector — larger interceptor

- **Tumbler** (`vector-tumbler.png`): irregular armored polyhedron inside one
  broken off-axis hoop; single corner cannon.
- **Lens** (`vector-lens.png`): unequal steel bowls expose a crescent of cells
  between them; tangential rim cannon.
- **Caliper** (`vector-caliper.png`): two unequal deep jaws around an empty
  volume, with one cannon at the short jaw.

## Mortar — larger area denial

- **Bell** (`mortar-bell.png`): floating vertical bell with one launch aperture
  through its narrow neck.
- **Crucible** (`mortar-crucible.png`): armored shell on one flank and open
  charge rack on the other; one broad forward throat.
- **Flywheel** (`mortar-flywheel.png`): thick vertical ring with a displaced hub
  and battery magazine occupying one quadrant.

## Bastion — larger frontline anchor

- **Vault** (`bastion-vault.png`): rounded armored roof over a compact heavy
  lower mass, with one missing corner exposing the cell wall.
- **Torus** (`bastion-torus.png`): tilted armored ring around a displaced cannon
  core, open over batteries on one side.
- **Cohort** (`bastion-cohort.png`) — *composite*: four blunt unarmed spherical
  drones dock around a central cannon orb.

## Relay — larger network coordinator

- **Armillary** (`relay-armillary.png`): three incomplete unequal rings orbit a
  displaced violet core on different axes.
- **Loom** (`relay-loom.png`): unequal vertical rails twist around a suspended
  core and irregular energy threads.
- **Chorus** (`relay-chorus.png`) — *composite*: nine capped relay cells assemble
  into an incomplete rising arc around one emitter.
