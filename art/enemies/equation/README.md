# The Equation — enemy concept set (first pass)

> **Superseded.** The current direction is [`cold-iron/`](cold-iron/README.md):
> one weapon mount, drone scale, bare unpainted metal, and volumetric rather
> than aircraft-shaped hulls. This first pass is kept for reference.

Fifteen visual concepts: three asymmetric machine-made variants for each of five
core combat frames. These are unmanned constructs driven by distributed
computation and electricity. They have no cockpit, canopy, windows, pilot seat,
or human-readable face.

## Shared art direction

- Match the player-ship family: stylized premium game concept render, chunky
  bevelled hard-surface panels, cream ceramic armor, charcoal machinery,
  restrained saturated accents, and occasional gold mechanical rings.
- Break the player fleet's reassuring bilateral symmetry. Every construct must
  have a deliberately unequal silhouette, offset center of mass, mismatched
  appendages, or orbiting components.
- Keep each frame readable from an elevated three-quarter gameplay camera.
- Use one bright, non-anthropomorphic computation core or sensor aperture as the
  focal point. Avoid eyes, faces, mouths, insects, flesh, veins, tentacles, and
  decorative pseudo-mathematical text.
- Electrical links, projected vectors, missing spans, and floating plates may
  replace ordinary structural connections.

## 1. Needle — saturation unit

- **N1 / Offset Thorn** (`equation-needle-offset-thorn.png`): narrow
  tetrahedral dart; red core pushed off-axis;
  one long ceramic blade and two unequal stabilizers.
- **N2 / Split Prong** (`equation-needle-split-prong.png`): forked ram with one
  heavy prong and one abbreviated electrode; open electrical gap through the
  center.
- **N3 / Crooked Kite** (`equation-needle-crooked-kite.png`): swept triangular
  machine with a clipped side and one detached fin held by cyan energy.

## 2. Vector — interceptor

- **V1 / Broken Arrow** (`equation-vector-broken-arrow.png`): arrowhead hull
  with one long gun rail and one compact articulated barrel; amber predictive
  emitter.
- **V2 / Offset Caliper** (`equation-vector-offset-caliper.png`): hooked
  opposing arms of unequal length around an exposed green computation core.
- **V3 / Sheared Delta** (`equation-vector-sheared-delta.png`): half-delta
  attack frame with one broad armor wing and a skeletal weapon boom on the
  other side.

## 3. Mortar — area denial

- **M1 / Sidewell** (`equation-mortar-sidewell.png`): bulky hexagonal magazine
  with its payload drum mounted to one flank and a recessed yellow sensor hood
  on the other.
- **M2 / Bent Crucible** (`equation-mortar-bent-crucible.png`): compact crooked
  chassis carrying one oversized vertical launch chamber and three mismatched
  coolant vanes.
- **M3 / Orbit Rack** (`equation-mortar-orbit-rack.png`): low armored body with
  an incomplete ring of floating ammunition cells and a single offset launch
  tube.

## 4. Bastion — frontline anchor

- **B1 / Fallen Cube** (`equation-bastion-fallen-cube.png`): massive cuboid
  whose armor planes have translated out of alignment around a white-hot core;
  one side is dense, the other open.
- **B2 / Counterweight** (`equation-bastion-counterweight.png`): heavy cannon
  block on one side balanced by a remote floating armor slab and energy struts
  on the other.
- **B3 / Staircase Monolith** (`equation-bastion-staircase-monolith.png`):
  stepped stack of unequal rectangular masses, rotating frontal shield plane,
  and a low offset blue power aperture.

## 5. Relay — network coordinator

- **R1 / Broken Halo** (`equation-relay-broken-halo.png`): slim spindle with an
  incomplete antenna ring and nodes concentrated on one side; violet-white data
  arcs bridge the missing section.
- **R2 / Crooked Abacus** (`equation-relay-crooked-abacus.png`): offset central
  core with floating bars and unequal light nodes arranged like an alien
  computational instrument.
- **R3 / Signal Fork** (`equation-relay-signal-fork.png`): tall bifurcated
  transmitter, one armored branch and one skeletal branch, with a detached
  theorem halo tilted off-axis.
