# The Equation — cold iron modular kit

Twenty scale-neutral building blocks derived from the compact and larger
cold-iron concept passes. These are visual design references for assembling new
enemy silhouettes, not production-ready meshes.

## Assembly language

- All structural connections use the same **octagonal socket / plug** family.
- Hull metal stays unpainted: brushed steel, gunmetal, and dark iron.
- Amber marks ordinary power; cyan marks power transfer and propulsion; violet
  is reserved for Relay computation.
- Keep assemblies asymmetric and volumetric.
- Use at most one `kit-20` weapon hardpoint per enemy. It is intentionally
  unarmed so the attached barrel, lance, tube, or emitter can define the frame.
- Exposed power and structure are desirable. Do not cover every gap with armor.

## Armor

1. `kit-01-curved-shell.png` — layered quarter-sphere hull plate.
2. `kit-02-wedge-panel.png` — deep triangular slab for prows and flanks.
3. `kit-03-ribbed-shield.png` — curved five-rib shield or cage segment.
4. `kit-04-aperture-plate.png` — reinforced empty opening for a system module.

## Power

5. `kit-05-amber-cell.png` — single general-purpose energy canister.
6. `kit-06-cell-bank.png` — integrated six-cell rack and bus.
7. `kit-07-violet-capacitor.png` — Relay computation core in an incomplete cage.
8. `kit-08-conduit-junction.png` — unequal four-way powered connector.

## Structure and connection

9. `kit-09-spine-beam.png` — long backbone with three side sockets.
10. `kit-10-curved-rib.png` — single heavy arc for hooks, rings, or cages.
11. `kit-11-gimbal-ring.png` — incomplete pivoting hoop.
12. `kit-12-docking-collar.png` — male-to-female universal adapter.
13. `kit-13-truss-fork.png` — unequal Y branch for offset assemblies.
14. `kit-14-energy-bridge.png` — paired anchors joined without a solid strut.

## Propulsion and systems

15. `kit-15-compact-thruster.png` — short fixed propulsion unit.
16. `kit-16-vector-thruster.png` — spherical thruster in unequal gimbals.
17. `kit-17-reaction-wheel.png` — attitude-control flywheel and frame.
18. `kit-18-sensor-core.png` — faceted violet computation and sensor module.
19. `kit-19-cooling-bank.png` — staggered radiator-fin rack.
20. `kit-20-weapon-hardpoint.png` — empty universal single-weapon cradle.

## Example combinations

- **Spherical drone:** curved shells + gimbal ring + sensor core + vector
  thruster + one hardpoint.
- **Vertical machine:** spine beam + cell bank + cooling bank + compact thruster
  + one hardpoint.
- **Open Relay:** curved ribs + energy bridges + violet capacitor + reaction
  wheel + one hardpoint.
- **Composite anchor:** truss forks + docking collars + several self-contained
  modules around one central cell bank and one hardpoint.
