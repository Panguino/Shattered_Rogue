# Weapon Art Prompts

> **Parent:** [00_GAME_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md](../00_GAME_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) · **Style family:** Ace materials · **Catalog:** [art/weapons.json](weapons.json)

Starter trio. Each weapon is a **standalone hardpoint module** that bolts onto one gold ship pad. Same camera, materials, and gold rules as the player ships.

## How to use

1. Paste the full prompt into image gen (Ace `art/ace.png` + `art/ships/interceptor/ace.png` as style refs).
2. Check the **one-line silhouette**. If two weapons could swap names, discard.
3. Count **gold** — exactly one circular mounting collar. Extra gold = discard.
4. Count **mod wells** — exactly four empty crystal sockets, not gold, nothing inserted.
5. Tripo later: Smart Mesh **6,000 tris**, export **GLB 2K**. Mount socket on the gold collar, facing down.

Gold on a weapon is **only** the mount collar that matches a ship hardpoint. Modifier wells are cream/charcoal crystal bays.

## Shared rules (gameplay, not the picture)

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ship slot | one gold weapon hardpoint |
| Levels | 5 |
| Own cap | 3 of the same type |
| Mod slots | L1=0, L2=1, L3=2, L4=3, L5=4 |
| Death | Keep what you entered the run with. Lose unstashed finds. |
| Stash | End of each level: stash forever, or keep using it on this run. |

## Laser Cannon

**Pulse** · starter · The current Ace pulse gun — hot-orange bolts, rapid fire.

*Silhouette:* long cream pulse barrel on a gold collar, fat crimson housing, hot-orange muzzle

```
3D game-ready concept of a SINGLE standalone spaceship weapon module, isolated on a clean light-gray studio background. No ship, no hangar, no scenery.

CAMERA: high 3/4 isometric from above-front-right, same product-shot camera as the crimson Ace interceptor catalog image. Entire weapon fully in frame, centered, no cropping. Slight downward angle like a top-down arcade game. Orthographic isometric product shot.

STYLE: Same locked ART FAMILY as Ace — StarCraft Terran + Ratchet & Clank / Fortnite / Astroneer toyetic. Bright colors, slightly low-poly, thick panel lines, chunky readable shapes, faint edge wear. SAME materials language as Ace: crimson-red plates, cream/white plates, charcoal mechanical recesses, stylized toy look. NOT photoreal, NOT grimdark, NOT sleek anime mecha.

WEAPON IDENTITY: Laser Cannon. ONE-LINE SILHOUETTE: a long cream pulse barrel on a gold circular collar, with a fat crimson housing and a hot-orange muzzle. If it looks like a missile box or a tesla coil, it is wrong.

SHAPE: compact fighter hardpoint gun, sized to bolt onto Ace's wing gold pad — not a capital-ship turret. Circular gold mounting collar at the BOTTOM of the weapon (the only gold on the whole object). Underside of the collar has a dark hexagonal plug that would seat into a ship's gold hardpoint. Above the collar: a chunky crimson-red rectangular receiver/housing with cream cheek plates. A long forward cream barrel with charcoal cooling fins along the sides, ending in a single circular muzzle. Inside the barrel and at the muzzle: hot orange energy glow (warm orange like Ace's engine bells, RGB-ish 255, 56, 4). Small charcoal heat vents. Thick panel lines. Chunky toy proportions, slightly low-poly.

MOD SLOTS: exactly 4 empty hexagonal crystal wells in a row on top of the crimson housing. Cream bezels, dark empty glass, nothing inserted. These wells are NOT gold. Count them: 4 empty wells.

GOLD RULE: gold exists ONLY as the single circular mounting collar at the base. No gold barrel rings, no gold wells, no gold screws.

Lighting: bright studio key, soft fill, punchy rim light. Low-poly stylized 3D product render.

DO NOT INCLUDE: a spaceship, a second weapon, hangar, stars, nebula, planet, text, logo, watermark, photorealism, grimdark, people, hands, missiles, rockets, lightning bolts, copper coils, extra gold circles, crystals already plugged in, flame exhaust, cropped weapon.
```
